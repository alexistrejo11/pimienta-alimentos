## Access and Workforce

- Registration, approval, login, refresh, logout, role management, and headquarters assignment support staff access.
- Employee profiles, attendance, work schedules, hiring and termination workflows, imports, and exports support HR operations.
- Contracts, payroll records, periods, payments, debts, and summaries support employment administration.

## Operations and Commercial Work

- CRM opportunities and projects support pipelines, milestones, tasks, lifecycle actions, summaries, and imports or exports.
- Tasks support assignment, status, and checklists.
- Headquarters records support location management and the POS configuration attached to each site.
- File resources and company file management provide uploads and time-limited access to stored assets.
- Notifications provide administrative records, statistics, and manager-facing logs.

## Inventory

- Item catalog, location trees, stock, and movement history expose the current inventory picture.
- Purchase, sale, transfer, return, scrap, adjustment, approval, completion, and cancellation workflows record inventory transactions.
- POS sales, waste, restock, and cancelled-sale events can project into location inventory records.

## POS Device Integration

- Administrators manage operators, devices, enrollment codes, incidents, and reports.
- A device enrolls once, refreshes its credentials when needed, retrieves a bootstrap or incremental changes, and submits event batches.
- Event responses distinguish accepted, duplicate, rejected, and review-required outcomes so the device can safely retry or escalate a record.
