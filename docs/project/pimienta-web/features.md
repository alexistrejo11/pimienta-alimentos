## Public and Staff Experiences

- Public pages communicate the company profile, services, commitments, quality and hygiene practices, and legal information.
- Authentication provides sign-in and registration before access to the staff workspace.
- The workspace groups dashboard, employees, CRM opportunities and projects, payroll, tasks, locations, contracts, and files.

## POS Central

- Administrators manage the master POS catalog, location-level inventory, devices, enrollment codes, operators, incidents, and consolidated reports.
- Managers are limited to the locations assigned to them.
- Operational screens surface sales, waste, cash cuts, and synchronization incidents received from POS devices.

## Integration Status

The routed workspace is broader than the API integrations verified in the existing frontend tracking documents. Areas with partial or pending API usage are intentionally not described as complete; the backend remains the contract source for those flows.
